# Your hiring experience: questions for Salman

Short answers are fine, in Bangla or English. Each answer becomes a Slide labelled "আমার অভিজ্ঞতায়". Skip anything you don't want on screen.

## Employers' expectations (topic 2)
1. When a CV for a junior analyst role reaches you, what do you look at in the first 30 seconds?
2. Name three skills that make you shortlist a fresher, and one thing that makes you reject a CV straight away.
3. Which skill do candidates claim most often but fail to show in the test?
4. Excel vs SQL vs Power BI vs Python: what order would you tell a fresher in Bangladesh to learn them in, and why?

## AI at work (topic 3)
5. How does your team use AI tools day to day: writing SQL, documentation, cleaning data, summaries?
6. Do you have one real (anonymised) story of AI output that looked right but was wrong? Wrong numbers, a wrong join, a made-up column…
7. Does your team have any rule on what data can't be pasted into an AI tool?

## Portfolio (topic 4)
8. What makes a portfolio project stand out to you? What makes one look copied from a tutorial?
9. GitHub, Kaggle, LinkedIn posts, a personal site: which do you actually open?

## Interviews (topic 6)
10. What are your team's interview stages, from CV to offer? Roughly how long is each, and who runs it?
11. What does your SQL or written test look like (level, time, topics)? One example question would be great; we can change the numbers.
12. What are the three most common mistakes you see in interviews?
13. One question you always ask, and what a good answer sounds like.

## Paths and pay (topics 5 and 7)
14. In your experience, which path is easiest to enter in Bangladesh right now, and which pays best after 3 years?
15. Can we show salary ranges? If yes, from the job posts only, or also your own estimate (labelled)?
