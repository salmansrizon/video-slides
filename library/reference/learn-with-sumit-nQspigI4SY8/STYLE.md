# Style reference: Learn with Sumit, "AI দিয়ে Software বানিয়ে আয় করবেন কীভাবে?"

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQspigI4SY8 (39:51). The motion graphics are 00:00–04:40, plus inserts at ~09:20 ("OPTIONAL") and ~09:30 ("READ IT"); the rest is screen recording. `frames-*.jpg` show one frame every 10 s; `key-frames.jpg` enlarges 12 of them.

## The look

- **Ground:** near-black (#09090b) with a faint square grid; lots of empty space; one focal element per frame.
- **Cards:** dark (#121215–#18181b), 1px border (#27272d), 20–26px radius, an icon left of a semibold title.
- **Pills:** small rounded name tags; a white pill with an avatar for the hero (Rafi), a coral pill for the second character.
- **Characters:** illustrated busts in a circle with a white ring, name pill below.
- **Icon tiles:** rounded squares, blue fill at 12% with a blue border and blue line icon.
- **Status:** red ✕ dots and green ✓ dots beside list items.
- **Accent:** yellow for numbers and emphasis ("3–4 MONTHS", M1–M4 tiles); lime for the one positive hero (TRUST).
- **Stamps:** rotated red outline boxes with spaced caps ("SENSITIVE", "RENTED").
- **Kinetic type:** heavy uppercase words with a hand-drawn scribble accent ("PATIENT DATA", "READ IT").
- **Motion:** elements arrive by moving or popping in from oversize, hard cuts between beats, connectors drawn as lines.
